## Theme & Description
A responsive pet adoption website designed to connect loving homes with rescued animals in need of shelter and care.

## Website Pages
- `index.html`: Homepage featuring the mission statement, banner image, search bar, and quick contact details.
- `petsgrid.html`: Available pets gallery featuring interactive pet profile cards with adopt buttons.
- `contactpage.html`: Contact Us page with adoption inquiries and shelter contact information.
